The opportunity

A concrete milestone, a working product, and a short runway to make it count.

Recovery Tracker is a continuity-of-care platform for outpatient surgery. Roughly 90% of recovery happens at home, unsupervised — surgeons see a patient for a fifteen-minute follow-up while the real risks (infection, poor healing, missed instructions) play out over three weeks. Recovery Tracker closes that gap: an AI recovery assistant for patients and a real-time dashboard for surgeons.

The product is built and entering its first clinical pilot with a Beverly Hills plastic surgery practice — the platform's clinical launch, and the milestone this raise is built around.

Recovery Tracker has been bootstrapped to this point. This raise is a small, short-term bridge to reach launch at full strength — not operating capital, only the last mile before going live. Principal plus 5% interest is returned in full, on the terms below.
